Problem

Outreach was manual: every lead was qualified, followed up on and entered into the CRM by hand, for hours every day.

System

An end-to-end pipeline that replaces manual outreach with intelligent workflows. Leads are qualified automatically, follow-ups sequenced and all data synced to Salesforce, with no human input at any step.

Result

Eliminated hours of daily manual outreach. Scaled lead engagement without adding a single person.
