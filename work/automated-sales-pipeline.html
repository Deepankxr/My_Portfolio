Problem

A US-based nonprofit was qualifying leads, sending follow-ups and updating Salesforce by hand. The same outreach steps had to be repeated for each new lead.

What I built

I connected lead qualification, follow-up sequencing and CRM updates in one pipeline. n8n coordinates the steps, OpenAI supports qualification, Supabase holds workflow data and Salesforce receives the CRM updates.

Outcome

The system replaced recurring manual outreach steps and kept lead records in sync. Hours saved, conversion lift and error rates were not measured in this case study.
