Problem

E-commerce teams were entering invoice details by hand across varied document formats and order flows. The useful fields had to be extracted and checked before they could be stored.

What I built

I used n8n to coordinate invoice ingestion, OpenAI to extract structured fields and MongoDB for order validation and record storage. The workflow turns incoming invoices into structured entries checked against order records.

Outcome

The workflow automated extraction, validation and database entry in the documented invoice flow. Document throughput, extraction accuracy and hours saved were not measured for publication.
