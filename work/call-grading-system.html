Problem

Sales and support coaching depended on managers listening through call recordings. Reviewing calls by hand made it difficult to apply the same rubric consistently.

What I built

I built a workflow that ingests calls, produces transcripts and scores them against a custom rubric. n8n coordinates processing, OpenAI supports the analysis, and Supabase stores results for review. The workflow surfaces coachable moments and recurring patterns.

Outcome

Call review became a repeatable workflow that surfaces coaching signals for managers. Time saved, scoring accuracy and coaching outcomes are unmeasured here.
